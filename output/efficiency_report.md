# Full-scan vertex finding on simulated E07 events

Generated by `./run.py evalreport` (e07-g4-hypernuclei). Signal: Geant4 Xi- + 12C -> LL6He + 4He + t with weak decays, rendered with the calibrated image model v3 and embedded into random real tohoku-v1 views (background, fog, beam and noise are real). Candidates: e07-fullscan method A (merge_vertex_slices, production and relaxed cuts) and B' (branch points).

Matching: one-to-one, 3-D distance in the developed emulsion. Depth conventions for method A candidates: **edge** = distance to the nearer end of the candidate z range, **inclusive** = zero inside the range, **best** = z_best (slice where most tracks cross) as a point. Each cell: efficiency (chance level from 10 random shifts of the truths by 400-1000 px).

## Reading the tables

- Only the excess over the chance level measures vertex finding: with ~800 method-A candidates per real view a 30 um tolerance is reached by chance in most cases, hence 6 and 12 um here.
- The dz comparison shows how method A performs if the physical focal step is 3.0 or 1.5 um per slice; it does not measure the step itself (the image model was tuned to real views at 3.0 um).
- z_best is pulled towards the middle of the image stack for vertices near its ends (section 3); a correction or a normalization of the crossing counts near the stack ends would sharpen it.
- Section 4 has 5 events only.
