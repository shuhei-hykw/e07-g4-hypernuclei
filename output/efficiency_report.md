# Full-scan vertex finding on simulated E07 events

Generated by `./run.py evalreport` (e07-g4-hypernuclei). Signal: Geant4 Xi- + 12C -> LL6He + 4He + t with weak decays, rendered with the calibrated image model (v19 / v20 in section 0, v3 in sections 1-5) and embedded into random real tohoku-v1 views (background, fog, beam and noise are real). Candidates: e07-fullscan method A (merge_vertex_slices, production and relaxed cuts) and B' (branch points).

Matching: one-to-one, 3-D distance in the developed emulsion. Depth conventions for method A candidates: **edge** = distance to the nearer end of the candidate z range, **inclusive** = zero inside the range, **best** = z_best (slice where most tracks cross) as a point. Each cell: efficiency (chance level from 10 random shifts of the truths by 400-1000 px).

### Ranking of B' branch points on the real special events (`./run.py specialsrank`)

Match: XY (px) and stage z (um) tolerances of the clicked vertex as in the section headers (24 stage um = 30 um at 3.8 um per 3 um stage step). On real events the multiplicity ranks well for most events (unlike the simulation, where the simulated vertices rarely produce high-multiplicity branch points), the darkness for others, and their combination ranks the true vertex better than the shifted control in all nine events.

Best rank (ties averaged) of a B' branch point matching the clicked vertex, rank fraction in brackets (smaller is better), number of matching points and XY / z offset of the best one; control: median [16 %, 84 %] of the rank fraction over 20 random shifts of the truth (common to all scores; no match counts as 1, 'none' = such controls); p = fraction of controls at least as good as the truth. At the strict tolerance most controls have no matching point at all, so the comparison with the control median is weak there; the integer rank against the method-A rate (770 per view) is the more useful figure.

Caveats: the darkness image model was checked against the IBUKI heavy tracks, so this is not a fully independent test; a match only means a high-ranked point near the vertex, not that the tracks of the vertex are connected to it (not checked here).

## Reading the tables

- The excess over the shifted-truth chance level is a measure of association, not an efficiency by itself; with ~800 method-A candidates per real view a 30 um tolerance is reached by chance in most cases, hence 6 and 12 um here. A stronger control is the method run on the same real views before embedding (section 1, background control; `./run.py efficiency --background-candidates`).
- The dz comparison shows how method A performs if the physical focal step is 3.0 or 1.5 um per slice; it does not measure the step itself. Calibrating the image model under either step could not distinguish them (output/dz_comparison.md).
- z_best is pulled towards the middle of the image stack for vertices near its ends (section 3); a correction or a normalization of the crossing counts near the stack ends would sharpen it.
- Section 4 (5 events per dz) is indicative only; the 20-event comparison is the basis for B' vs A: B' (all branch points) has a larger excess over chance at 6 um than method A but ~40 times more candidates per view, and cuts on the branch multiplicity remove the signal faster than the background.
- Synthetic v3 images give ~40 method-A candidates per view, synthetic v4 ~795, v11 ~905 and v14 ~875, close to the ~770 of real views: these backgrounds reproduce the candidate rate of real data, so their efficiencies can be compared with the embedded ones.
- The physical step, 3.8 +- 0.2 um per slice from the layer extent, is discussed in output/dz_comparison.md; the earlier ~1.5 um from grains counted along beam tracks is withdrawn.
